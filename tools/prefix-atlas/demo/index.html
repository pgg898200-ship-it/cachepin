What counts as a hit?

On arrival, each request reuses its longest contiguous cached prefix. After completion, pages are touched from tip to root, leaving shallow pages most recent. The replay starts empty and processes requests in order. It models complete pages and excludes GPU scheduling, decode allocations and concurrent pinning.
